Research · Blue-team · Hardening

AppArmor — RCE Containment / Hardening

Reproduced a trivial PHP RCE (system($_GET['cmd']) as www-data) and confined the Apache process with AppArmor: deny /bin/** x, deny /usr/bin/** x, deny network inet/inet6. Re-test returned Unable to execute command: Permission denied with audit DENIED exec /bin/sh; remediated root cause with an allowlist and php.ini disable_functions.

Why it mattered: Demonstrates compensating control vs. root-cause fix — runtime containment at the LSM boundary buys time while source hardening (allowlist + disabled primitives) closes the path.

Research · Reverse Engineering · Namespaces

Linux — /etc/machine-id Trial & Mount-Namespace Investigation

Reverse-engineered a Linux trial binary (licensed_app_linux, isolated VM) that bound trial state to /etc/machine-id: strings revealed the path, strace -e openat,read showed openat(AT_FDCWD, "/etc/machine-id") and read 33 at startup. Isolated bypass with unshare -r -m + mount --bind /tmp/fake-machine-id /etc/machine-id restored Trial Active without touching the host.

Why it mattered: Proves filesystem trust as insufficient on Linux and maps the per-process virtualization primitive end-to-end, with server-signed HWID-bound licensing mitigations and detection via strings | strace.
