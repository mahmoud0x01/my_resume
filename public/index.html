About Me

A practical security perspective shaped by both testing and operating real systems.

I am a junior penetration tester building toward security analyst and infrastructure-security roles. My hands-on assessment work covers authorized web/API and network testing; labs and coursework extend that foundation into Linux, Windows, Active Directory, and defensive validation.

I test only within authorized scope and use responsible disclosure for security research. 20+ bug-bounty Hall of Fame recognitions—including DigitalOcean, Pinterest, Verisign, and Dell, plus many programs through Bugcrowd—a Top 5 / 100 result in Bastion’s internal CTF, and two technical internships—including one in penetration testing—support this foundation.

My Software Engineering degree adds structured development, documentation, and automation skills. I publish security notes and build tools and applications that make security findings easier to reproduce and explain.
